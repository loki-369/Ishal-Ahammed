A bit about me

My best projects came from small problems. Printing Polaroids for events was slow, so I wrote OrmaPrint. Our event needed a website, so InnoFesta got its Harry Potter theme. The fuel calculator was only for me until my friends kept asking for more features.

I like to keep things useful first with a simple interface. I ship early and change things once real people have used them.
